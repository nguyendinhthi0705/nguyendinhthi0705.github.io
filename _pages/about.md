I've spent 20 years in software — from developer to startup co-founder, principal engineer, technical architect, and now Senior Solutions Architect. For the past two years I've been recognized as AWS Vietnam's GenAI Champion.

My focus is **agentic AI in production**: architecture, evaluation, context engineering, security and isolation, and cost and latency optimization. I also help modernize business-critical workloads — core banking, SAP, and legacy databases — on the cloud. I believe the best way to learn is to build, and to share what you build.
